\chapter{Glossary}
\label{app:glossary}

\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.5em, style=nextline]
  \item[Admission Control] Pre-playback verification requiring $\mathrm{WCET} \le Q / f_s$, hardware readiness, and valid state before transitioning to \texttt{PLAYING}.
  \item[Audio-Visual Shim Principle] The B-System architectural invariant mapping each 3D kinematic action one-to-one onto a zero-byte procedural DSP function.
  \item[B-Sound] The Virtual Digital Signal Processor (Virtual DSP) of B-System / BTRON~3.20, providing 13 DSP primitives, 23 canonical Sound Shims, and an S16\_LE stereo VirtIO output contract.
  \item[Biquad Filter] A second-order recursive IIR filter defined by five normalized difference-equation coefficients $(b_0, b_1, b_2, a_1, a_2)$.
  \item[Dihedral Group $D_{12}$] The 24-element symmetry group of 12-tone pitch classes generated by transposition $T_k$ and inversion $I_k$.
  \item[Hopf Fibration ($S^3 \to S^2$)] A fiber bundle decomposing the 3-sphere into linked great circles, projected stereographically into $\mathbb{R}^3$ as the \emph{Lil~Cu~64} background.
  \item[Insert Position] One of two per-track slots (\texttt{ins0}, \texttt{ins1}) or the master limiter slot populated from a closed catalogue of eight statically pooled DSP algorithms.
  \item[Neo-Riemannian $PLR$ Group] The group generated by Parallel, Leittonwechsel, and Relative involutions acting simply transitively on the 24 major and minor triads.
  \item[One-Shot Voice] A percussive or chime program (\texttt{shot = 1}) whose duration is intrinsic to the instrument and ignores MIDI Note-Off.
  \item[\texttt{OrchView}] The plain-old-data snapshot of \emph{Orchestra} desk state published atomically across a seqlock from the media layer to the UI layer.
  \item[Quantum ($Q = 32$)] The atomic 32-frame synthesis block (\texttt{RENDER\_BLOCK}) defining the real-time deadline $T = Q/f_s$.
  \item[Schroeder Hall Reverb] A shared reverberation unit composed of four parallel damped comb filters feeding four series all-pass diffusers per stereo channel.
  \item[Sound Shim] A stateless, zero-byte procedural synthesis procedure $S(t_0, \mathbf{p})$ combining analytical oscillators, exponential envelopes, and filters.
  \item[VirtIO Sound Contract] The five-call output-only hardware interface (\texttt{open}, \texttt{write}, \texttt{close}, \texttt{is\_ready}, \texttt{get\_queued\_bytes}) carrying interleaved little-endian 16-bit stereo PCM.
  \item[WaveShaper] A memoryless non-linear transfer function $C_k(x)$ introducing controlled harmonic saturation via a pre-sampled 1024-entry table.
\end{description}
